Chromora 1.4.1 remembers more of your workspace and fixes highlights appearing on hidden template colors.

## Fixes and improvements

- **Hidden colors stay hidden.** General highlights, incorrect-pixel markers, and missing-pixel outlines now respect the template color filter.
- **Color sorting survives reloads.** Color Filter remembers the applied sort field, ascending or descending order, and **Show unused colors** preference.
- **Your workspace returns.** The main window, Templates, Settings, and Color Filter remember their positions and whether they were minimized. Templates, Settings, and Color Filter reopen only when they were left open.
- **Minimized windows keep their full size.** Moving a minimized Color Filter preserves its expanded dimensions and the sizes of its separate layouts. Restored windows stay within the screen, including after expanding near its lower edge.
- Existing settings and template storage are preserved. These changes adapt selected improvements from Blue Marble 0.93–0.95 to Chromora's current interface and storage format.

## Validation

- 65 automated tests passed, including 12 browser scenarios for window persistence with regular and reduced animations.
- 87 checks passed against the built userscript, including a fresh runtime restoring saved window states and color sorting.
- Syntax and build-contract checks passed.

## Install or update

Download **Chromora.user.js** from this release's assets, open it in your userscript manager, and confirm the update. Refresh [Wplace](https://wplace.live/) afterward.

**SHA256SUMS.txt** contains the checksum of the attached userscript.
